# Doodle film: three prompts

1. **"A kid who loses a thing and finds it."** 10 s. Shots: Pip pats pockets in a wide street (cat watches) → medium: the cat drops a key at his feet, startle + "o" mouth → close: key held up, doodle hearts orbit one by one → wide: both walk home at dusk. Caption plates: "where is it?", "oh!", "found it."
2. **"How a good habit starts" (explainer).** 12 s. A tiny seed-spark falls, Pip plants it (rays draw on), the days pass as the sky turns sky → lilac → night → sky with a sprout growing (staggered build), final plate "small, daily". One focal point per shot.
3. **"Launch teaser for a tiny app."** 8 s. Close on a doodled phone drawn on line by line (`ink` with `p`), Pip pokes it, a star pops out, three orbiting doodles = the three features (one word each, ≥ 60 px), scribble wipe to the product name plate.

**Vertical:** any of the three works as a 9:16 Short when re-staged for the tall frame (closer, stacked, the action travelling up or down); see `templates/vertical.html` and the vertical section of `STYLE.md`.
