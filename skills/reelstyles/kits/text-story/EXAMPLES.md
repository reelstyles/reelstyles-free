# Chat text story: example prompts

1. **The wrong number.** "A mum texts her son 'dinner at 7?'. He answers 'who is this?'. Her photo of the kitchen table shows three plates. Twist card: 'WE ARE ONLY TWO.'" 9:16, 12 s, one photo (`window`-style warm colours), reaction `eyes` on the last message.
2. **The group-chat roast.** "Four friends plan a trip; one never confirms; typing dots appear and vanish three times; finally 'I got the tickets' with a photo. Twist card: 'THEY WERE FOR A DIFFERENT TRIP.'" Use a long pause with dots, history of three messages.
3. **The late reply.** "Message sent 11:49 pm: 'are you up?'. Reply arrives with the time 3:12 am: 'sorry, just saw this. who is this?'. Twist card: 'IT WAS YOUR OWN NUMBER.'" Jump the timestamps, tint the card `bg #7BE0C3`, `hi #FF7A59`.
