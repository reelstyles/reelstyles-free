# Whiteboard explainer: example prompts

1. **"How does compound interest work?" (60 s, money).** Title SNOWBALL → a graph with a straight "saving" line and a curved "investing" line → a magnet coin rides the curve → highlight and circle the gap → eraser wipes the title, pull back to the whole board. Shots: hold, whip, push, pull-back.
2. **"Why is the sky blue?" (75 s, science).** Open on a huge `?` shrunk into the sun (circle + rays), a sun ray drawn as an arrow splitting into three colours (ink / sig / hot), a person (`person`) looking up, the formula written term by term while the voice says it, the answer circled twice. Use `dashed` for light paths and a duet of two pens.
3. **"How a startup makes money" (90 s, business).** A timeline drawn left to right (the pen leads the camera), boxes (`box`) for cost / price / profit with `arrow`s between them, a magnet coin dropping into each box with a clack, the eraser rewinding a bad plan, a final `bars` chart and a highlighter under the one number that matters.

Vertical: every prompt also works as a 9:16 Short (about 30-45 s). Stack the title on two lines, draw tall diagrams, whip down the board between ideas and end on a push into the answer; see `STYLE.md` "Vertical 9:16".
