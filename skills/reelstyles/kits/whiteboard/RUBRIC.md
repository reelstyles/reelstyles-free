# Whiteboard explainer: rubric (/20)

Score each 0-2. Ship at ≥ 15 with no 0.

1. **One physical board.** Everything is marker on one board; no hands, no blocks of faded-in text, no pasted art.
2. **Writing is the explanation.** Terms appear in stroke order exactly when said; a drawing per sentence, simplest geometry that carries the idea.
3. **Stroke quality.** Wobble, chisel width, taper, dry texture visible at 100 %; lines do not look like vector polylines.
4. **Colour logic.** At most three marker colours, each with one held meaning; one `hot` accent per region.
5. **Camera.** Moves with purpose (hold, whip, push, ride, pull-back); no cuts on the board; blur only on whips; nothing half-cut by a push.
6. **Props alive.** Pens hop and park, magnet drops and slides, eraser leaves a ghost; shadows react to lift.
7. **Layout.** One focal point per shot; bottom 12 % free for captions; labels clear of lines; nothing runs off the frame.
8. **Readability at phone size.** Labels ≥ 40 px cap at 1080p, captions ≥ 44 px, contrast of ghost marks never competes with the content.
9. **Sound.** Every stroke sounds; foley under the voice; silence is used once on purpose.
10. **Motionmaxxing pass.** No dead holds over 1.2 s (something always writes, drifts or pushes); easing never linear; hierarchy clear (title > label > ghost); motion has purpose, not decoration; ending is a deliberate image.

## Vertical 9:16 checks (when the film is 1080 x 1920)
Also pass all of these; any "no" caps the score at 14.
- Full frame composed for vertical: no letterbox, pillarbox or scaled-16:9 look, no empty or flat band at the top or bottom.
- Nothing important in the UI zones: text, captions, numbers inside x 90-940, y 250-1470 (top 250, bottom 450, right 140 px stay clear).
- Text ≥ 44 px, at most about 4 words per line, nothing clipped by the frame or by a push-in.
- The subject reads at phone size: big shapes, thick strokes, one focal point per shot.
- No dead holds (freezedetect with d=1.2 finds nothing); the camera uses the height (whip down, ride up, push in).
